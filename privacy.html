Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the DoSaS publisher's informational website and skills-only Reader Edition do, and distinguishes them from the third-party services that host the site and chat experience.

Publisher: Joseph Omega · Effective date: October 20, 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the public DoSaS Reader Edition information pages and the publisher-supplied conceptual content and instructions used by the Reader Edition in ChatGPT. It does not replace the privacy policies of OpenAI, GitHub, an email provider, or any other service the reader separately uses.

2. Information collected by this website

The website is a static collection of informational pages. The supplied site files have no sign-in, contact form, payment processing, advertising scripts, independent analytics scripts, or publisher-set tracking cookies. The website code does not request a reader's name, address, or account data.

However, GitHub Pages, as the hosting platform, may process technical information such as IP addresses, device/browser information and request logs under GitHub's own privacy practices. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Conversations in ChatGPT

The Reader Edition is a skills-only plugin. It contains educational instructions and conceptual reference materials, not a publisher-operated chat server or external retrieval tool. The published Reader Edition does not contain the complete companion manuscripts and is not configured to transmit conversation prompts to a separate DoSaS-controlled endpoint.

ChatGPT conversations, accounts, model behavior, storage, security, retention, and optional data-use settings are handled by OpenAI under its applicable policies and user controls. The publisher cannot manage or delete a reader's ChatGPT conversations. Readers should refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy and the ChatGPT Privacy Center information.

4. Voluntary support correspondence

If you email the public support address, the publisher may receive the sender address, name or other identifiers you voluntarily include, the content of the message, and related correspondence. That information is used to understand and respond to the request and, when appropriate, to address issues raised.

Retention of support emails: The publisher keeps support correspondence in the email account until choosing to archive or delete it manually. Archiving removes a message from the active inbox but does not erase it: archived messages may continue to be stored in the email account until the publisher deletes them. There is no promised automatic deletion date for this correspondence.

Support correspondence may also be processed or retained by the email provider under its own policies, including any applicable backup or recovery periods. Do not email sensitive personal data or private manuscript text.

5. External services and links

This site links to OpenAI and GitHub documentation. Visiting those services is governed by their own terms and privacy notices. This version of the website includes no third-party advertising, embedded chat widget, web analytics provider, or marketing mailing list.

6. Reader choices and requests

For ChatGPT conversation records and ChatGPT data controls, use OpenAI's account settings and privacy mechanisms. For website-hosting data, consult GitHub's privacy resources. For voluntarily submitted DoSaS support correspondence, contact the publisher at JoeOmega@ymail.com with your request. Rights and remedies depend on applicable law and the services involved.

7. Updates

If the publisher adds analytics, a contact form, external tools, new information-sharing practices, or other material changes, this policy should be updated before those features are enabled. The effective date will be updated when a revised policy is published.
