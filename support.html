Support

Questions about the DoSaS Reader Edition, manuscript-protection boundaries, accessibility, or this informational site can be directed to the publisher.

Contact the publisher

Publisher: Joseph Omega

Public support email: JoeOmega@ymail.com

Use this address for reader inquiries, reports of inaccurate conceptual explanations, accessibility issues, and privacy questions. Response times are not guaranteed. Support messages may be kept until the publisher manually archives or deletes them; archived messages may still be retained. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What to include

  • The subject of your question or the type of issue.
  • A short explanation and, if relevant, an example of the Reader Edition's public response.
  • Whether the issue concerns an incorrect concept, a technical display problem, or a privacy request.

Please do not send passwords, financial or medical records, unpublished manuscript passages, or sensitive personal information in an ordinary support email.

What the public edition can and cannot provide

The public Reader Edition can discuss the general DoSaS / Pattern-Law / LoRR framework, its terminology, symbolism, philosophical implications, and original illustrative metaphors.

It cannot provide manuscript quotations, chapter- or scene-specific explanations, passage searches, close paraphrases, or reconstructed literary scenes. The public service is not a channel for requests to override those boundaries.

ChatGPT account or platform problems

For problems signing in, managing ChatGPT account settings, or other ChatGPT platform services, visit the OpenAI Help Center. The DoSaS publisher does not operate the ChatGPT platform.
