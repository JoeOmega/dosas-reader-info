Central idea

Complementarity without sameness.

DoSaS examines how differentiated relational functions can become reciprocal. Its central distinction is between equal human worth and identical relational roles—two ideas the framework does not treat as interchangeable.

Axis · masculine-coded

Direction and containment.

Projective action, dependable boundaries, initiative and responsibility for consequences.

Field · feminine-coded

Receptivity and meaning.

Relational openness, integration, trust, responsiveness and the vulnerability of surrender.

“Different contributions need not be interchangeable to be reciprocal.”

This is a newly composed summary of the framework, not a quotation from either DoSaS manuscript.

Public Reader Edition boundaries
